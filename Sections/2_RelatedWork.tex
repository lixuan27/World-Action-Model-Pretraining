\section{Related Work}
\textbf{Video priors for embodied learning.}
Video generation learns temporal structure from visual data \citep{wan}. UniPi uses generated videos
as plans from which actions are recovered \citep{unipi}. Cosmos Policy incorporates actions, future
observations, and values into a pretrained video model through latent frames \citep{cosmos}. These
approaches establish practical routes from generative priors to control. JAM focuses on how action
learning influences the video representation during embodied pretraining, with explicit controls
over the connections carrying information and gradients.

\textbf{Joint action and world prediction.}
Unified World Models (UWM) jointly diffuse actions and observations, sample their noise levels
independently, and obtain policies and dynamics models through conditioning \citep{uwm}. JAM builds
on this joint-distribution perspective and uses rectified-flow training
\citep{flowmatching,rectifiedflow}. Its study centers on a pretrained video stream coupled
throughout its depth to an agent stream. Separating visibility from gradient routing permits
comparisons between reading world features and updating those features through action supervision.
Conditional sampling provides an additional use of the learned distribution; the representation
question motivates the experimental design.

\textbf{Action representations across embodiments.}
Robot policy pretraining aligns observations and language with commands across datasets and
platforms \citep{openvla,droid,bridge}. Visual trajectories offer another interface to interaction:
Any-point Trajectory Modeling learns motion representations for policies, and Track2Act converts
predicted point tracks into executable motion \citep{atm,track2act}. EgoDex supplies human
manipulation video with tracked hand poses \citep{egodex}. JAM incorporates image-space consequences
into its joint generative objective, alongside motor commands where available. The corresponding
experiments examine command--consequence complementarity and transfer across embodiments under
explicitly matched observation and annotation conditions.

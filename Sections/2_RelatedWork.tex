\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\Paragraph{World and video pretraining.}
Denoising generative models made video synthesis a scalable sequence modelling
problem \citep{ho2020ddpm,rombach2022ldm,esser2024sd3}, and open video
generators such as the Wan family \citep{wan2025wan} now supply a transferable
prior over scene dynamics. Robotics consumes that prior along two routes. The
planning route generates a future observation and recovers a command from it
with a separate inverse dynamics module or a goal-reaching controller
\citep{du2023unipi,black2024susie,wu2024ivideogpt}. The pretrain-then-attach
route trains on web video and then fine-tunes an action head on teleoperation
data \citep{wu2024gr1,cheang2024gr2}. Latent world models compress dynamics into
a learned transition function and plan inside it \citep{hafner2023dreamerv3}.
Each route optimises a model of observation dynamics during pretraining, so the
action that produced the observed change is marginalised out of the objective.
\sys{} keeps the video prior and makes the action a generated variable of the
pretraining objective itself, so the pretrained representation is shaped by the
mapping from an action to its visual effect.

\Paragraph{Action-conditioned world modelling.}
Conditioning a video model on an action sequence gives a simulator that scores a
proposed command, and pairing it with a policy gives a planner. Joint diffusion
over video and action moves closer to the present formulation by placing both
quantities in one denoising process \citep{zhu2025uwm}. \sys{} differs in three
respects that the experiments isolate. The two noise levels are drawn
independently, so the training distribution covers a plane of noise-level pairs
and the action-conditioned model, the inverse dynamics model, and the policy
become three paths through one trained network rather than three training
recipes. The exchange between streams happens at every backbone layer inside one
shared attention space and carries gradient in both directions, so the action
objective shapes the world representation as well as reading it. Coupling is exposed as an axis with declared
degrees of freedom, which distinguishes joint modelling from an auxiliary action
prediction task attached to a frozen trunk under matched parameters and data.

\Paragraph{Action representation for embodied learning.}
Vision-language-action policies attach an action decoder to a pretrained
backbone and supervise it on demonstrations, using autoregressive action tokens
\citep{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla}, flow-matching action experts
\citep{black2024pi0,intelligence2025pi05,bjorck2025groot,liu2025rdt}, or action
chunking \citep{chi2023diffusionpolicy,zhao2023act,octo2024}. Training one such
policy across many platforms requires a shared action interface, and large
cross-embodiment corpora make that interface a practical necessity
\citep{oxe2024,khazatsky2024droid,agibot2025}. Motor commands remain
embodiment-specific even after such an interface is imposed, which motivates
representations defined directly in the image: point trajectories predicted from
video supply an embodiment-independent intermediate for policy learning
\citep{wen2024atm,bharadhwaj2024track2act}, and modern trackers make such labels
cheap to produce at scale \citep{doersch2023tapir,karaev2024cotracker}. Latent
action pretraining takes the complementary route of inferring pseudo-commands
from unlabelled video \citep{ye2025lapa}. \sys{} places both levels inside one
joint process: the command stream carries the platform-specific control signal,
and the consequence stream carries the displacement that the command produces in
the image, which supplies grounding on egocentric human footage and gives a
probe of representation content that transfers across embodiments.

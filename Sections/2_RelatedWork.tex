\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\Paragraph{Video generative models as control backbones.}
Denoising generative models turned video synthesis into a scalable sequence
modelling problem \citep{ho2020ddpm,rombach2022ldm}, and the transformer
denoisers that carry it \citep{peebles2023dit,esser2024sd3} now underpin open
video generators such as
the Wan family \citep{wan2025wan}, Cosmos \citep{nvidia2025cosmos}, CogVideoX
\citep{yang2025cogvideox} and Stable Video Diffusion
\citep{blattmann2023svd}. Robotics adopted these generators along two routes.
The planning route generates a future observation and recovers the action with a
separate inverse dynamics model or a goal-reaching controller
\citep{du2023unipi,black2024susie,wu2024ivideogpt,bruce2024genie}. The joint
route asks one network to denoise video and actions together, so a single
forward pass produces both quantities \citep{zhu2025uwm}. Both routes descend
from latent world models that learn a compact transition function and plan
inside it \citep{hafner2023dreamerv3}, and they replace that compact latent with
the observation space itself. Video pretraining on
web footage followed by action fine-tuning also improves policies whose action
head is conventional \citep{wu2024gr1,cheang2024gr2}. Latent action modelling
extracts pseudo-action labels from unannotated video and pretrains a policy on
them \citep{ye2025lapa}. \sys{} follows the joint route and treats the coupling
between the two streams as a variable to be measured.

\Paragraph{Vision-language-action policies.}
The competing family attaches an action decoder to a pretrained
vision-language model and supervises it on teleoperation data. Autoregressive
action tokenisation \citep{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla}, optimised parallel
decoding \citep{kim2025openvlaoft} and flow-matching action experts
\citep{black2024pi0,intelligence2025pi05,bjorck2025groot,liu2025rdt} span the
current design range, and earlier action-chunking policies established the
supervised recipe these systems refine
\citep{chi2023diffusionpolicy,zhao2023act,octo2024}. These policies inherit
language grounding from the backbone and treat the observation stream purely as
an input. \sys{} keeps the language pathway and adds a visual
prediction target, and one variant of the framework restores an explicit
understanding stream by attaching a frozen vision-language expert
\citep{bai2025qwen25vl} to the joint attention.

\Paragraph{Pretraining corpora and cross-embodiment action spaces.}
Large-scale robot corpora now aggregate hundreds of embodiments and tasks
\citep{oxe2024,khazatsky2024droid,agibot2025}, and human egocentric collections
add orders of magnitude more footage at the cost of action labels
\citep{grauman2022ego4d,hoque2025egodex}. Training one policy across such
heterogeneous sources requires a shared action interface, which existing systems
obtain either by tokenising per-embodiment action vectors or by learning a
separate head for each platform. \sys{} adopts a fixed wide action layout with
per-dimension supervision masks, so a gripper platform and a dexterous hand
occupy disjoint slots of one head and every source contributes gradient only
where it carries a measurement. This interface is what allows action-free
egocentric footage to enter the same optimisation as fully labelled robot
trajectories.

\Paragraph{Representation choices for prediction.}
Self-supervised image and video encoders produce features that predict semantics
well \citep{oquab2024dinov2,simeoni2025dinov3,assran2025vjepa2}, and variational
autoencoders produce compact reconstructive latents that generative models
denoise efficiently \citep{kingma2014vae,rombach2022ldm}. World models for
control have been built on both, and the two families differ in latent width by
more than an order of magnitude. \sys{} isolates width from semantics by
inserting a small frozen reducer that compresses a semantic latent to the channel
count of the reconstructive latent, which turns the comparison into a controlled
one.

\Paragraph{Frameworks, controlled studies and evaluation harnesses.}
Reproducible robot learning depends on shared data formats
\citep{cadene2024lerobot}, composable configuration \citep{yadan2019hydra} and
memory-efficient distributed training \citep{rajbhandari2020zero,paszke2019pytorch},
and on benchmark suites that stress distinct competences
\citep{liu2023libero,nasiriany2024robocasa,chen2025robotwin2,zhang2024vlabench}.
Prior work releases a policy together with the harness that scored it. \sys{}
releases the axes as well, so the framework exposes every architectural,
representational and scheduling decision as a selectable component, and the
study that follows reports each axis under one protocol. The sparse multi-expert
attention pattern that one of our coupling variants uses is the mixture of
transformers construction \citep{liang2024mot}, applied here across the video and
action modalities.

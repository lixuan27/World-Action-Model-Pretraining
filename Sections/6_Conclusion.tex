\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

World pretraining supplies a model of how the visual world evolves, and embodied
interaction needs a model of how the agent participates in that evolution. This
report closes that gap by construction rather than by an added prediction task.
Joint Action-World Modeling places the future observation and the future action
chunk in one continuous flow-matching process, gives each stream its own noise
level, and lets the two streams exchange keys and values through one shared
attention space at every layer of the backbone.

Two consequences follow from the formulation alone and hold independently of any
measurement. Independent noise levels give the training distribution full
support on the square of level pairs, which makes the sampler a deployment-time
choice and turns one trained network into a policy, an action-conditioned world
model, and an inverse dynamics model at once. Those same three paths are
measurement instruments, because the world-clamped ray reads how much action
information the world representation carries and the action-clamped ray reads how
strongly the generated future follows an intervention on the action. The
coupling therefore becomes a single experimental axis with three declared
degrees of freedom, and the gradient degree of freedom separates a representation
that the action objective shapes from a representation that an auxiliary head
merely reads.

The formulation extends to action consequence without a second method. Defining
consequence as the displacement and observability of world keypoints places the
grounding signal in image coordinates, which reaches egocentric human footage
that carries no action label and gives a probe of representation content that
transfers across embodiments. Consequence enters as a third generated stream of
the same joint process, shares the action noise level, and inherits the
visibility setting under study, so the model tree stays single.

The implementation is verified at the level the current runs support. The
uncoupled forward pass reproduces the official backbone implementation to the
bit, gradients are blocked exactly where the coupling specification says they
are, and a resumed run repeats no optimiser step. Normalisation statistics for
both battlegrounds are computed and frozen into the cache fingerprint. The
closed-loop and representation measurements of \sref{sec:exp} are pre-registered
with their arms, their floors, their statistical test, and their acceptance
criteria fixed in advance, and the targets printed in this report record what
each measurement is expected to show rather than what it has shown, and
published policies supply the external comparison on each benchmark.

The core insight the formulation offers is therefore stated as a testable
proposition rather than as a result: if coupling helps, it helps by changing what
the world representation encodes about action and consequence, and the three
paths through the noise plane are sufficient to decide that question without
appealing to a benchmark average. \sref{sec:plan} names the instrument that
decides each question and the floor that would retract it, and the honest
boundary is recorded beside it: the study runs in simulation alone, and its
corpus matches the largest published recipe in size while differing from it in
composition, because the evaluation suites stay out of pretraining.

\subsection{Experimental setup}
JAM begins embodied pretraining on DROID \citep{droid}, BridgeData V2 \citep{bridge}, and
EgoDex \citep{egodex}, then admits RoboMIND \citep{robomind} Franka and UR5e at update 8,400.
Figure~\ref{fig:mixture} separates the consumed mixture from the next data admissions.
The 6.02-billion-parameter model combines a public video initialization with a newly initialized
agent stream. Foundation training uses eight H200 accelerators and global batch 512;
episode-level holdouts separate training windows from open-loop checks. Benchmark adaptation
uses the designated training partitions, with LIBERO-Plus and LIBERO-PRO reserved for evaluation.
Appendix~\ref{app:setup} records the complete data inventory, schedules, and evaluation protocols.

The evidence snapshot contains foundation optimization through update \FoundationSteps{},
a completed 20,000-update LIBERO adaptation, and frozen-world diagnostics at update 5,100.
Training behavior appears in Figure~\ref{fig:foundation}; benchmark tables retain source-reported
baseline values and reserve a blue row for JAM evaluation. Paired comparisons fix demonstrations,
adaptation budgets, and environment resets. Success measures closed-loop control; action readout,
consequence displacement, and input sensitivity assess representation content. Controlled
pretraining and scaling displays retain explicit target markers while their measurements are
pending. Probe sampling, uncertainty, and evidence boundaries appear in Appendix~\ref{app:controls}.
